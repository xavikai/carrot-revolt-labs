    FROM THE LAB TO THE THEATRE

    The Teatro Real in numbers

    The lab simplifies the machinery of the Teatro Real (Madrid): fewer bars and platforms, the same ideas.

    1. The stage is 2.54 m above the street. The stage house rises 47 levels above the ground and goes down to "cota -16", 24 m under the stage.
    2. 68 motorised bars of 20 m, parallel to the audience, lift up to 750 kg each (evenly spread). Point loads hang from 35 "puntos" of 150 kg and 40 "puntuales" of 250 kg.
    3. The platforms of 18.20 × 3.5 m carry up to 24 t; four of them also move horizontally. A set of wagons has four units.
    4. 18 goods lifts of different kinds bring the scenery in: a typical set weighs 10–15 t, a heavy one up to 20 t.
    5. In Blender: model the stage house to scale and put a camera at the eye height of the worst seats. The Camera view shows at once what the masking hides.
