    FROM THE LAB TO THE LIVE DESK

    What carries over

    In a real production, the director calls shots and the technical director switches them. The preview monitor lets them inspect the next camera before it goes on air. This exercise pauses each moment so you can practise that decision.

    1. Before the show: agree on the route, camera positions, shot sizes and cues with the camera operators.
    2. During the walk: keep a wide safety shot ready. Use entrance and side cameras to vary the view without losing the model.
    3. At the end: C3 performs a vertical tilt from feet to head while the model holds position. After the turn, return to a safe view.
    4. Presenter: C1 remains framed on the presenter for introductions and handoffs; it does not need to be on air continuously.

    Teaching setup: the presenter stands beside the runway on stage left. C2–C5 positions are a practical proposal for this exercise; a real venue needs a site visit and camera rehearsal.
