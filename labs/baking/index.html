    KEY IDEAS

    How a bake works

    For every texel of the image, Blender finds the point of the low poly with those UVs and casts a ray towards the high poly. What the ray finds is written into the texel.

    Selected to Active
    Bake from the selected objects (high poly) to the active one (low poly). Without it, an object bakes itself.
    Extrusion
    How far out the rays start. It must enclose every detail that sticks out of the low poly.
    Cage
    A copy of the low poly, inflated along averaged normals, used as the start of the rays. It avoids gaps at hard edges.
    Max Ray Distance
    How far the rays can travel (0 = no limit). A limit avoids hitting far parts of the model.
    Tangent space
    The normal map stores directions relative to each UV island: R = right, G = up, B = out. Flat is (0.5, 0.5, 1).
    Margin
    Extends the islands outwards so filtering and mipmaps don't pick up the empty background.
    BACK IN BLENDER

    Baking in Blender, step by step

    Baking uses the Cycles render engine. The lab shows the same settings, with a simplified crate and a fast ray caster instead of Cycles.

    1. Unwrap the low poly with unique UVs (no overlaps) and put UV seams on its hard edges. Add a material with an Image Texture node pointing to a new image, and keep that node selected: it is the bake target.
    2. Set the render engine to Cycles. In Render Properties › Bake choose the Bake Type.
    3. Select the high poly, then Shift-click the low poly (active). Tick Selected to Active, set Extrusion or a Cage object and a Max Ray Distance.
    4. Press Bake. Save the image (Image › Save): baked images are not saved with the .blend file.
    5. Normal maps: Non-Color + Normal Map node. For Unreal, bake with Swizzle G −Y or flip green on import.
    6. For colour, bake Diffuse with only Color. Keep a Margin of 16 px or more.

    The same bake, other names

    Both programs cast one ray per texel from the low poly to the high poly. What changes is where the settings are and what they are called.
