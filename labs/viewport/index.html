Move the view, then move the objects.

Your first hour in Blender: orbit, pan and zoom around a scene, frame what you need and jump to the axis views. Then select objects and move, rotate and scale them with G, R and S, along an axis and with exact values. The keys are the real ones: they work the same in Blender.

    KEY IDEAS

    The view is not the scene

    Orbit, pan and zoom move your eye. G, R and S move the objects. Mixing them up is the first mistake of every beginner.

    Orbit · Pan · Zoom
    Middle mouse button, Shift + middle, the wheel. Orbit turns around the centre of the view; pan moves that centre.
    Frame
    Numpad . frames the selection and puts the orbit centre on it. Home frames everything.
    Axis views
    Numpad 1, 3 and 7: Front, Right and Top; with Ctrl, the opposite side. Numpad 5 switches Perspective and Orthographic.
    Selection
    Click, Shift + click, B for a box, A for all, Alt A for none. The active object is the last one clicked: lighter orange.
    G · R · S
    Grab (move), rotate and scale. Then X, Y or Z for an axis, Shift + axis to lock it, a number for an exact value.
    Confirm · Cancel · Undo
    Click or Enter confirms, right-click or Esc cancels. Ctrl Z undoes what you already confirmed.
    IN THE REAL BLENDER

    The same keys, a few settings

    Everything here works the same in Blender's Layout workspace, with the mouse over the 3D Viewport.

    1. No middle button or no numpad? Edit › Preferences › Input: Emulate 3 Button Mouse (Alt + left button orbits) and Emulate Numpad (the number row works as a numpad).
    2. Laptop touchpad: two fingers orbit, Shift + two fingers pan, pinch zooms. The gizmo at the top right also orbits, pans and zooms.
    3. View › Viewpoint and the ` pie menu give the axis views without a numpad; View › Frame Selected and Frame All do the same as Numpad . and Home.
    4. The Select menu has All, None, Invert and Box Select. The Outliner (top right) lists every object and selects them too.
    5. Object › Transform has Move, Rotate and Scale; Object › Clear resets them (Alt G, Alt R, Alt S).
    6. Edit › Undo History jumps back several steps. The sidebar (N) shows Location, Rotation, Scale and Dimensions, and you can type into them.
